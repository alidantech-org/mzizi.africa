# Why your MCA matters more than you think

Most people can name the President. Fewer can name their MCA, yet the MCA votes on the county budget: the clinic stock, the murram on your road, the bursary fund.

## Three things to do
1. Find your ward on the **Places** tab.
2. Look at your MCA's attendance bar.
3. Look at what was built in your ward, and who was paid to build it.
