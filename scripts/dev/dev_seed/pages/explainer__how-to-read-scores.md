# How to read the performance bars

The bar under a leader shows one number from 0 to 100 and how that leader compares with others in the same job.

- For members of an assembly it is the **attendance at recorded votes**: votes taken part in divided by votes held.
- For leaders who do not vote, it is the **share of projects in their area that are completed**.
- If nothing is recorded, the bar is empty and says so. An empty bar is not a bad score.

A bar measures one thing. It does not say whether a leader is good.
