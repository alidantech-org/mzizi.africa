# County governments

Since 2013 Kenya has 47 county governments. Each has an **assembly** that makes county laws and an **executive** led by a **Governor**.

Counties are responsible for health facilities, county roads, early childhood education, agriculture, markets and county planning, and they receive a share of national revenue.
