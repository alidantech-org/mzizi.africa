# Appointed leaders

These leaders were chosen rather than elected. Cabinet Secretaries and Principal Secretaries are appointed by the President; judges are recommended by the Judicial Service Commission; commissioners are chosen by a panel and approved by Parliament. The Speakers are elected by the members of their house.
