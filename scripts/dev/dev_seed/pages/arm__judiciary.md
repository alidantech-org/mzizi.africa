# The Judiciary

Courts decide disputes between people, between people and the state, and whether a law or an action is allowed by the Constitution.

## The courts, from the top
1. Supreme Court
2. Court of Appeal
3. High Court, and the Employment and Labour Relations Court and the Environment and Land Court
4. Magistrates' courts

The **Judicial Service Commission** recommends who becomes a judge, so that judges are chosen on merit.
