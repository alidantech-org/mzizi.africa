# Your rights in the Bill of Rights

Chapter Four lists the rights every person has. A few that matter in daily life:

- **Equality and freedom from discrimination** (Article 27)
- **Life, dignity and security** (Articles 26, 28, 29)
- **Freedom of expression and the media** (Articles 33, 34)
- **Access to information** held by the state (Article 35)
- **Fair administrative action** (Article 47)
- **Health, housing, food, water and education** (Article 43)

The courts can order the state to respect these rights.
