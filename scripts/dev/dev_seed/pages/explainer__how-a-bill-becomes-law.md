# How a bill becomes law

1. **A bill is published.** The Cabinet, a member of Parliament or a county may propose one.
2. **First reading.** The bill is introduced in the National Assembly (or the Senate if it concerns counties).
3. **Committee.** A committee reads the bill, invites the public to give views and reports back.
4. **Second and third reading.** Members debate and vote.
5. **The other house.** The bill goes to the second house to be passed there too.
6. **The President.** The President signs it into law or sends it back with comments.

Every vote members take is listed on this app, under **Parliament**.
