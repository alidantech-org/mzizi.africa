# Where public money comes from and where it goes

- **Collection.** The Kenya Revenue Authority collects taxes.
- **Sharing.** Parliament decides how much goes to the national government and how much to counties.
- **Equalisation.** The Equalisation Fund helps the poorest regions.
- **Constituencies.** The NG-CDF spends on schools, clinics and roads in every constituency.
- **Checking.** The Controller of Budget approves withdrawals and the Auditor-General audits what was spent.

On this app, open **Money and projects** to see what was allocated, what was built and by whom.
