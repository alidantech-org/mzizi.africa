# The Executive

The Executive runs the country day to day. At national level it is the **President**, the **Deputy President** and the **Cabinet**, helped by Principal Secretaries and the civil service. At county level it is the **Governor**, the **Deputy Governor** and the County Executive Committee.

## What it does
- Carries out the laws Parliament makes
- Writes the budget and spends the money Parliament approves
- Runs ministries, the police, the defence forces and public services

## Who it answers to
Parliament can question and censure the Executive, and the courts can check that it acts within the law.
