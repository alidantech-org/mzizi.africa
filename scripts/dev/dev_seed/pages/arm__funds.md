# Funds, authorities and state corporations

Beyond ministries and commissions, the law creates agencies for particular jobs: collecting tax (KRA), regulating transport (NTSA), regulating public buying (PPRA), or holding money for development (the **NG-CDF** and the **Equalisation Fund**). Most are created by an **Act of Parliament**.
