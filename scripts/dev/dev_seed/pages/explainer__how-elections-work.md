# How Kenyan elections work

On one day every five years, a voter casts up to **six votes**:

1. President (with the Deputy President as running mate)
2. Governor (with the Deputy Governor as running mate)
3. Senator
4. Woman Representative
5. Member of Parliament
6. Member of the County Assembly (MCA) for the ward

The **IEBC** runs the election and announces the results. The last general election was on **9 August 2022**.

Some members are **nominated** from party lists to make sure women, youth and persons with disabilities are in the assemblies.
