# Elected leaders

These are the leaders you voted for, plus the running mates who share their ticket (the Deputy President and the Deputy Governor).

In one election every five years, you vote for a President, a Governor, a Senator, a Woman Representative, a Member of Parliament and a Member of the County Assembly (MCA).
