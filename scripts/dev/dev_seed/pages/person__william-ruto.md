# William Ruto

William Ruto was sworn in as the fifth President of Kenya on 13 September 2022 after winning the August 2022 election. From 2013 to 2022 he was the Deputy President.

As President he is the Head of State and Government and the Commander-in-Chief of the Defence Forces.
