# The Legislature

The Legislature makes laws, decides how public money is shared and spent, and watches over the Executive.

- **Parliament** has two houses: the **National Assembly** (349 members) and the **Senate** (67 members).
- Each of the 47 **county assemblies** makes county laws and checks the Governor.

## Why two houses?
The National Assembly speaks for the people. The Senate speaks for the counties and protects devolution.
