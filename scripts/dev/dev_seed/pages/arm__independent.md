# Independent commissions and offices

Some jobs are too important to leave to politicians: running elections, auditing government accounts, protecting human rights. The Constitution creates **commissions** and **independent offices** for them, and says that nobody can direct them in their work.

Examples: the IEBC (elections), the Auditor-General (audits), the Salaries and Remuneration Commission (pay), the Public Service Commission (hiring) and the Commission on Revenue Allocation (sharing money with counties).
