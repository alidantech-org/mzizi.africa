# What is devolution?

Devolution means that some decisions are made, and some money is spent, by governments close to the people: the **47 counties**. It began after the March 2013 elections.

## Who does what?
| National government | County government |
| --- | --- |
| Defence, foreign affairs, police | Health facilities and ambulances |
| Main roads and the national budget | County roads and street lighting |
| Universities and national schools | Early childhood education and polytechnics |
| Immigration, citizenship | Agriculture, markets, county planning |

## The money
At least 15% of national revenue goes to the counties. The **Commission on Revenue Allocation** proposes the formula, the **Senate** decides it, and the **Controller of Budget** checks the spending.
