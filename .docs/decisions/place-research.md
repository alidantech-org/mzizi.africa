# Decision: place research and its integrity rules

Date: 3 November 2026 (lane H4). Implements TSK-EBA9E2370C55 and TSK-4864E3B1AD5E; builds on DEC-PLACES-AND-OFFICE-PATTERNS, DEC-CLAIMS-AND-CONFLICTS, DEC-ENTITY-MATCHING and DEC-AUTO-APPROVAL.

## Decision

- **A generated expectation is never evidence of a building.** An expectation says a presence should exist. A place needs an attribution to a source document, an occupancy needs one too, and an expectation becomes `identified` only through an attributed occupancy whose place is verified (or whose point two independent publishers agree on).
- **The IEBC constituency offices PDF** (published by the IEBC on its own site) is read by word position, using the column header of each county block. Constituency names are scored with rapidfuzz against the constituency units of the matched county: a match needs a similarity of at least 90 and a lead of 6 over the runner-up, and two rows claiming one constituency are both left unlinked. The agent verifies a link at 95 or above; between 90 and 95 the place is `under_review` and resolves nothing. A ward that scores 95 or more is only a hint in the report. Rows for the county-level IEBC office link to the county. Places carry address text and landmark text only, no point.
- **Refill sources** register through `register_refill`: `site_documents` (addresses found in the registry's crawled documents of the sites linked to the institution; nothing is fetched), `wikidata_place` (P625 coordinate of a Kenyan item whose label matches closely, CC0) and `osm_nominatim` (ODbL 1.0 recorded on the source document, an identified agent, at most 0.2 requests a second, which is inside the Nominatim limit of one a second). A found value is a proposed claim with an attribution.
- **OpenStreetMap Nominatim is blocked by its robots.txt.** The gate refuses `/search` on `nominatim.openstreetmap.org` (a `RobotsDisallowed` stop on the first request). The engine does not bypass robots.txt for it as it does for the documented Wikimedia API endpoint; the handler and the site registration stay in place and the source stays unused until the owner decides (for example a self-hosted Nominatim or an Overpass-based route that the OSM policy permits).
- **Claims are applied, not copied.** `apply_place_claims` writes a point when two independent publishers agree within 500 m, or one publisher of trust 0.8 or more is the only claimant; an address when two publishers agree or one trusted publisher is alone. A value already in the place is never overwritten, a disagreement stays proposed and is reported, and a point outside Kenya is ignored.
- **Duplicate places** are scored on name, address and point and written as `flagged` rows of `engine.match_candidates` with kind `place` (migration `1795500000400`). A name alone is capped at 0.7 and marked `nameOnly`; a pair more than 2 km apart is discounted. Nothing is merged.
- **Moves, splits, merges and closures** end an occupancy (`moved` or `closed`) and link the successor; the place and old occupancy stay, the expectation follows a move and becomes `ceased` on a closure. A source document is required.
- **Enrichment** adds a structure, floor, wing, room, unit or service point only when the supplied source quote names it and the parent kind allows it; a building type is a classification the source also names.
- **Contacts** are stored at exactly one scope and never copied to other scopes. They are `restricted` unless they are a switchboard or general line published by the institution itself on a non-person scope (and an email is not a `first.last` form); a person scope is always restricted. Contact lookup falls back from a place to its parents and then to an occupant, but **never crosses subjects**: a place shared by several bodies lends nothing from any occupant unless the caller names the subject.
- **Integrity rules in the database engine** (`checkIntegrity`): every place and occupancy has an attribution; an expectation resolved by an occupancy needs that occupancy attributed and still in force; occupancy and expectation targets exist (already present); no expectation resolves to a nonexistent occupancy (already present); generated expectations, offices and institutions match the newest version of their pattern document; a person's contact is never public and a public contact has an attribution. Each has a failing-case test in `test/place-integrity.test.ts`.

## Consequences

- Of the 290 IEBC constituency offices, those whose published constituency name matches a unit in the database get an identified expectation. Names the database spells differently (for example Garissa Township, Mbeere North and South, Dagoretti North and South, Bureti, Sisiria, Tindiret) stay unlinked and are listed in `.data/reports/constituency-offices.json`; fixing the geography names or adding aliases is the way to link them, not loosening the threshold.
- The landmark places (national bodies, county governments and assemblies) exist as unverified seeds with text addresses. They resolve their headquarters expectations only after a second source agrees on a point. With Nominatim blocked, only Wikidata can supply one, and a single Wikidata claim (trust 0.6) is not enough.
- No contact is imported from a live source in this lane: none of the preserved documents publishes a switchboard that the importer could attribute. The import path and its visibility policy are tested.
