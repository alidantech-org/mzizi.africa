# DOMAINS — Knowledge Mzizi connects

Mzizi connects African heritage, history, geography, language, society and civic life through evidence, people, institutions, place and time. Domain names describe research responsibilities; they do not assert that every proposed schema or feature is implemented.

## Knowledge areas

- **Heritage and culture** preserve material and intangible histories, practices, memory and identity.
- **History** explains chronology, continuity and change across political and social systems.
- **Geography** represents places, changing boundaries and spatial relationships without forcing historical evidence into modern borders.
- **Linguistics and migration** help explain language relationships, contact, movement and settlement.
- **Governance, politics, law and accountability** explain authority, competition, legal foundations and public oversight.
- **Finance and procurement** show how public power raises, allocates and uses resources.
- **Civic infrastructure and services** connect institutions, locations, projects, facilities and delivery.
- **Sources and evidence** preserve provenance, uncertainty, disagreement and change across every area.

## Current design scope

The [schema design catalogue](../_plan/schema-redesign/README.md) assigns schemas to Operational, Phase 01 or deferred Phase 02. Phase 01 covers civic accountability, including Geography and geospatial querying. Broader scientific, historical, heritage and statistical proposals remain deferred. These assignments guide design; they do not claim full runtime implementation.
