# Jurisdiction dossier template

Copy for each new country, fill it in with an accountable reviewer, and mirror the result in a `website.dossiers` row (applicable laws with the date each was checked, treaty status, launch stage, open questions). The stage moves research, pilot, production only by a named human; the collection gate requires pilot or production.

1. **Entity and footprint**: the legal entity, where people, servers, processors and data subjects are.
2. **Regulators and repositories**: privacy regulator, access-to-information body, cyber authority, the official legislation repository, and the consolidated current text of each law below, with commencement and amendments.
3. **Laws**: access to information; data protection; cybercrime and computer access; copyright and database rights; public records and reuse; sector secrecy (procurement, tax, health, education, electoral, courts); defamation and publication risk.
4. **Treaties**: status on the African Union treaty list (signature, ratification, entry into force) and any regional instrument.
5. **Obligations**: registration, representative or DPO, DPIA, localisation, transfer mechanism.
6. **Source policy**: allowed categories and methods, prohibited material, personal-data exclusions and lawful bases, rate and identification policy, complaint and takedown procedure.
7. **Processors**: every AI and cloud provider that sees source content, its role, location, retention, training use, subprocessors and transfer basis.
8. **Pilot**: the first source inventory (licensed bulk data preferred), its assessments, and the conditions for resuming after an incident.
9. **Record**: reviewer, evidence, decision, conditions, next review date.
