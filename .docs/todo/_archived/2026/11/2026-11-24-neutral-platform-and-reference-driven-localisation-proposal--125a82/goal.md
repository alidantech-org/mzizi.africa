# Goal

Preserve the neutral-platform and reference-driven localisation direction as a reviewable planning proposal in the repository documentation. The outcome is one proposal that states why the database, API and engine stay free of country-specific content, how seed datasets and manifests carry that knowledge instead, and how the vector index, knowledge base, goals and data mappings derive from them. Success is measured by the proposal being linked from the plan index, marked as unapproved, and accurate against the repository rules in AGENTS.md.

The proposal also explains why a human or agent verifier must stay in the loop: neutral machinery cannot fully understand data on its own, so mappings are reviewed once per source and then reused, which keeps review effort small and auditable over time.
