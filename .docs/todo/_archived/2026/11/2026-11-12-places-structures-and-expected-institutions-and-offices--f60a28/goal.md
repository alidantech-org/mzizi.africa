# Goal

Model physical places and structures separately from organisations, and make every public body and every public post known in advance from patterns and seeds. The group replaces `organization.locations` with a place schema (places, structures, spaces, occupancies, postal addresses, contacts), links every organisation and institution to places through dated occupancies, and builds a catalogue of institutions and offices so that every expected seat exists as a record even before a holder or a location is found.

Success is measured by: the old locations table and any wrongly designed columns removed after their data is moved; every institution and organisation with at least one occupancy (expected or real); all ministries, state agencies, commissions and independent bodies seeded with their offices; patterned offices created for every county, constituency and ward; and reports showing which places and holders are still unknown so reinforcement can fill them.
