# Goal

Generate the repetitive part of every Mzizi frontend from Dryv contracts instead of writing it by hand. Mzizi will ship a public mobile app (`source/apps/app`), a public website (`source/apps/web`), an administrative portal (`source/apps/admin`, today's `source/apps/web`) and a developer portal (`source/apps/dev`). Each is a Dryv application contract that selects one shared theme, declares its destinations and views, and is generated by the official Dryv frontend packs.

Success is measured by what is generated and how much of it survives review: one theme contract is the only place colours, type, radii and shadows are defined, and both the Next.js and Flutter apps receive them from generation. Typed API clients and a first list-and-form journey are generated, while decorative and design-heavy screens stay hand-written. Capabilities Dryv lacks are added to Dryv itself, not worked around in Mzizi.

The existing `source/apps/mobile` and the landing page in `source/apps/www` are not touched until the owner approves.
