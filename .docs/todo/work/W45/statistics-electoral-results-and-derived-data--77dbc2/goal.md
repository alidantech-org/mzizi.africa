# Statistics, electoral results and derived data goal

## Goal

Make Mzizi able to answer quantitative questions about Kenya place by place and year by year: how many people live in a county, constituency or ward, how they are distributed by sex, age, ethnic community, religion and urban or rural setting, how connected and prosperous they are, how many voters were registered, how many votes were cast and for whom, and what follows from those numbers (population density, turnout, vote share, registered voters per polling area). Every figure must come from a named publication or system, keep its vintage (census count, projection or survey estimate), and link to the geography unit it describes.

## Why it matters

Geography, people, parliament and funds are now loaded, but nothing yet says how large a place is or how many people or voters it has. Statistics are published mostly by the Kenya National Bureau of Statistics (population and housing census, Kenya Integrated Household Budget Survey, Economic Survey, Statistical Abstract), the Demographic and Health Survey programme, the Communications Authority (internet and mobile), the Commission on Revenue Allocation, the IEBC (voter registers and results) and research institutions. Without a schema that carries dimensions, derivations and revisions, these numbers would be flattened and misread.

## Success criteria

- Population by geography unit for the 2019 census at county, constituency and ward level, with sex, and age and household breakdowns where published.
- Ethnic community, religion, internet and device access, education, employment and poverty figures loaded where the sources publish them, each linked to the unit, period, publisher and source document.
- Electoral registers and results (registered voters, votes cast, valid and rejected ballots, per-candidate votes, referendum options) for the 2013, 2017 and 2022 general elections at every level the IEBC publishes.
- Area in square kilometres per geography unit, and derived indicators (density, turnout, vote share) computed by the system, stored with their inputs, and recomputed when an input is revised.
