# Political parties, ORPP and IEBC data goal

## Goal

Model the Office of the Registrar of Political Parties (ORPP) as an institution, and record complete, linked data about political parties, aspirants and candidates, party funding and spending, and the IEBC's registers, boundaries and results, all linked to geography units, people and institutions by fuzzy matching, with every figure traceable to the PDF or page it came from.

## Why it matters

Parliament members and parties are loaded from the Parliament lists with only a party abbreviation. The ORPP publishes the register of parties with constitutions, logos and officials, the allocations from the Political Parties Fund and parties' audited accounts as PDFs; the IEBC publishes aspirants, registers, boundaries and results. Together they explain who competes for power and how it is financed, and they feed the electoral and statistical work.

## Success criteria

- ORPP recorded as an institution with its site, assessed and probed through the gate.
- Every registered party with its legal name, abbreviation, registration data, status, officials, constitution document and logo file.
- Political Parties Fund money to each party and each party's reported income and expenditure by year.
- IEBC aspirants and candidates, registers, boundaries and results linked to geography units and persons.
