# World Bank population series (sample data)

`SP.POP.TOTL-<ISO3>.json` are unmodified responses of `https://api.worldbank.org/v2/country/<ISO3>/indicator/SP.POP.TOTL?format=json&per_page=100` (indicator "Population, total", 1960 to 2025, last updated by the World Bank 2026-10-08), retrieved 2026-11-02 for Kenya, South Africa, Nigeria and Tanzania.

Licence: World Bank Open Data is published under CC BY 4.0 according to the World Bank's data terms of use; the API response itself carries no licence field, so the licence recorded with the document is marked as not confirmed by a human reviewer. The series aggregates figures from national statistical offices and the UN Population Division; it is a modelled national total, not a census table.
